Keep score for Oh Hell (also known as Oh Pshaw, Blackout, Nomination Whist, Kachuful and many other names) without pen and paper.

Enter each player's bid and the tricks they took; the app works out the scores, the running totals, the dealer, how many cards to deal and which bid the dealer is not allowed to make.

Every rule is a separate setting: how the number of cards dealt changes from hand to hand, how trumps are chosen, whether the dealer's bid is restricted, and exactly how bids, made bids, over-tricks and under-tricks score. Pick one of the included presets or build your own house rules.

Games are stored only on this device. There is no account and no network access.
